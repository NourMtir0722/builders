Builderland style board

Every asset spins on a turntable in day, golden hour and night, drawn by the same passes as the land. Colours come from src/tokens.js and are checked against the rules below. Drag any view to orbit all three. Click an asset to compare it up close.

Value strip

Every token in grey, light to dark. Ground should read light, darks only for ink, trunks and windows.

Approve the board

  • Golden hour reads warm with cool, tinted shade (not grey).
  • Night: windows, lanterns and the lab orb glow.
  • Value check: ground light, buildings mid, darks reserved.
  • Only accents pop. Nothing else shouts.
  • Each landmark has a silhouette you'd know at 64 px.
